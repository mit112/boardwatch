## T105 — Bounded heading context for bullet lists (DESIGN OWED)

**Do not hand this to an executor.** The ticket says design-first and **D-531 is the cautionary
case: T104's stated mechanism could not reach its own acceptance cases.** ~200–300 production
lines across three PRs. **Re-keys both hashes.**

**It owns a live regression:** after T104 merged, posting **261677** reads `ineligible` when its
bare-bachelor's arm clears.

**Cause.** `_SENTENCE_SPLIT` (`detect.py:46`) splits on `\n+` and on bullet markers, so
`split_units` (`:105-127`) returns isolated pieces and a heading is gone by the time its bullets
are read. A hedge, negation, jurisdiction or explicit OR stated in a heading never reaches the
bullet it governs.

**Red-first, reproducing today:**
```
"Nice to have:\n- 5 years of experience."   -> ineligible   want: eligible, preferred/unmet
  (inline control "Nice to have: 5 years…"  -> eligible   — the pair DISAGREE)
"You must meet either of the following:\n- A master's degree.\nOR\n- 5 years…" -> ineligible
"Requirements:\n- HS Diploma (or equivalent) AND 4+ yrs\n- OR Associate's AND 2+\n- OR Bachelor's"
                                            -> ineligible   (the 261677 shape)
```

**Three PRs.** **T105a** heading context inherited by following bullets until the next heading or a
blank-line break. **T105b** `Canada roles:` sets the jurisdiction the sponsorship rows scope to
(reuses the `jurisdiction_map` vocabulary at `rules.yaml:405`, `:410`). **T105c** an explicit `OR`
between bullets forms an alternative group rolled up as **any-of** — **there is no any-of today**,
only `exclusive_groups` and `refinement_groups` (`engine.py:258-296`, `:311-320`).

**The structural fact that sizes it.** `split_units` returns `list[tuple[int, str]]`; heading
