### T37 — a board's apply LOST to the lane thread's writes (SP2's first production defect; S–M)
Run 3's one error: `fmr.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/fmr/FidelityCareers: apply failed:
OperationalError('database is locked')`. The board was fetched in full (135 workday boards in
`fetch_cost`, 134 `board_scans` rows), then `apply_board`'s transaction could not get the write
lock inside `busy_timeout` (5,000 ms, `store/db.py:103`) and the whole snapshot was discarded;
coverage reports the board `unscanned`. **Timing pins it to the lane thread:** FidelityCareers is
board #11 in fleet order (stable across runs 2 and 3); its run-3 neighbours applied between
03:44:57Z and 03:45:51Z, and the lane thread wrote its 307 `lane` rows from 03:42:38Z to
03:45:41Z — one short transaction per company, back to back, 5.85 s of `apply_seconds`. SQLite's
busy handler is not fair, so a writer polling for the lock behind a stream of short transactions
can starve past the timeout. The store is WAL; a `mode=ro` reader cannot have caused this.
- **Reproduce first**, in a test: a stream of short writer transactions on one thread, one
  `apply_board`-shaped transaction on another, `busy_timeout` at the production 5 s — assert the
  second writer's failure before any fix. **Neither existing guard models this pair:**
  `tests/pipeline/test_two_writer_concurrency.py` races two PROCESSES of 200 tiny `insert_run`
  transactions each (no writer ever waits near 5 s), and `test_lane_stage.py::
  test_the_applies_never_overlap_because_apply_board_is_the_single_writer` proves the two LANES
  apply serially with each other — not with the coordinator's `apply_board` on the scan thread,
  which is the pair SP2 created.
- **Preferred fix: fetch concurrently, apply serially.** SP2's prize is the 346 s of lane FETCH
  under the scan; the lane APPLY is 5.9 s. `_run_lanes` still carries the comment "PARALLEL
  fetch, SERIAL apply — `apply_board` is the pipeline's single writer", which SP2 made false:
  `_run_lanes_stage` runs the whole of `_run_lanes` (fetch AND apply) on the daemon thread while
  the coordinator applies boards on the main thread. Split it: the thread returns the fetched
  `LaneResult`s; the join site (`pipeline/runner.py`, after the P5a preflight) applies them.
  That restores the comment's truth instead of tuning around it. **One stated consequence to
  decide, not discover:** SP2's docstring says a run refused at P5a "never joins this thread"
  and its rows still land in the background; with apply moved past the join, a refused run
  applies nothing from the lanes — pre-SP2 behaviour, and a refused run delivers nothing
  anyway. Say which you chose. A raised `busy_timeout` for the coordinator's apply is the
  fallback, and if taken it must be measured against the reproduction, not asserted.
