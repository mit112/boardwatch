**The harness exists; reuse it, don't rebuild it.** It lives at

  **`ineligible` with a quoted span, or any fit `no` (role_family / seniority / location)**. An

## #2 — T113 (RE-SCOPED) · Automatic, bounded refresh of the standing queue's gate readings

**The original ticket** is `TICKETS-2026-09-19-ASTRA-02.md` §T113 (stale gate NEGATIVES suppressing
their own repair; `top_cmd` hides and promotes on a historical read). **2026-09-22 widened it.** A
stale reading is not only a negative that suppresses; every re-key also strands the POSITIVES that
0-B's promotion needs.
- The original sizing was 97 of 984 standing leads with no current gate row. After run 470 it was
  **947 of 987**.
- The repair was done by hand (D-548) with `rejudge_backlog.py`. That script is the manual form of
  this ticket.

**What to build (design first, then code).**
- A refresh step in the daily run, after the gate judges the depth slate:
  - Take standing-queue leads (§0's population) that have **no current gate reading** under the
    gate's freshness key.
  - Judge them through the SAME `run_gate_stage` (it accepts any objects with `posting_id`) within
    a **per-run budget**.
  - Order **promotable holds first** (`no_requirements_found`, `experience_requirement`, the two
    holds a judge `eligible` releases), then the apply lane, then the rest.
- **Budget** is a new `[gate]` setting. `0` means off, and that is the multi-tenant default.
  - It is a scheduling knob, so it belongs OUT of `config_hash`, like `depth`. It changes which
    leads carry a verdict at the lane split, so it goes INTO `_ROUTING_RELEVANT`.
  - It is a new `Settings` field, so it has **four gated registration sites**: `cli/config_cmd.py`
    `_GATE_KEYS`; `tools/generalization/snapshots.py` (both dicts, plus the field class);
    `reports/manifest.py`; `core/settings.py`. `make check` catches a miss; narrow pytest does not.
- **Funnel.** Report it in the funnel's `gate` block: `refresh_candidates`, `refresh_sent`,
  `refresh_pending_after`.
  - Pending staying high across runs is the signal that the budget cannot keep up.
  - Any soft alert must sit ABOVE `_emit_morning` (the finalize-block ordering invariant).
- **Chunk the writes.** `run_gate_stage` commits once at the end, so call it per chunk. A timeout
  or kill must not discard a whole refresh.
- **The original half still stands.** `top_cmd` hides on a historical `ineligible` and promotes on a
  historical `eligible` (the T113 text cites `top_cmd.py:540-545`, `:700-703`, `:724-728`; line
  numbers drift, so re-find them). A stale negative should become a review hold pending re-read,
  not a suppression.

**Size, from the 2026-09-22 calibration.**
- A full standing queue is ~833 leads, about 64 calls at 13 per call: ~30–40% of the seat in one go.
- A budget of ~130 per run (10 calls, ~5–7% of the seat per run) drains a full re-key in about 7 runs.
- In steady state (no re-key) the refresh is near 0.

**Interactions.**
- **T152** removes `rules_hash` from the SENIORITY reading's key, so rules-only edits stop killing
  seniority readings. Verdict rows stay identity-keyed, so T113 is still needed.
- **T155** adds `effort` to the freshness key; T113 should respect whatever key exists.
- **Fail direction.** D-380 still holds: absent reads `unclear`/no promotion, never `no`.
- **Tests to keep green:** `tests/unit/test_rank_gate_filter.py:142`, `:116`, and
  `tests/unit/test_rank_verdict_tiers.py:107,136,153`. Also the `lane_decision` AST guard, which
  enumerates the five call sites.

**Discriminating tests owed.**
1. A fixture re-key followed by a run: the refresh restores a 0-B promotion that the re-key demoted.
2. The budget is respected.
3. Order: promotable holds come first.
4. A failed batch leaves its leads pending, never dropped.
5. Budget `0` means no refresh at all. This is the multi-tenant default.

**Done when.** A re-key's standing-queue damage heals without a hand-run script, within a stated
number of runs, visible in the funnel.

### T156 — Season-named graduation windows are unreadable (`TICKETS-2026-09-22.md` §T156)
- **Site:** `eligibility/resolve.py:614`, in the `graduation_window_required` branch.
  - `_yyyymm(detection.values["from_month"], …)` returns `None` for `Fall`/`Summer`, so the rule
    resolves `UNKNOWN`, "the stated window names a month this catalog cannot read".
  - The detector already captures the season token: posting 110519 arrives as
    `from_month='Fall', to_month='Summer'`.
- **Reach:** 457 of 260,581 open postings (0.18%). Measured in `.agent/2026-09-22b/notes.md:166`.
- **Needs a season → month table as catalog DATA, not code**, with a stated bound direction.
  Recommended: the low bound takes the season's FIRST month and the high bound its LAST month (the
  generous reading), since an over-tight bound turns a match into an `unmet` and deletes a job.
  Winter spans a year boundary, so state its month choice explicitly.
- **Controls:** an inverted window must still read "inverted", and an unknown token must still
  abstain.

### T157 — Company-tenure prose read as a years bar (`TICKETS-2026-09-22.md` §T157; D-544 "Half B")
- **Reach:** 49 of 407,150 unmet `experience_years` rows. Example: *"With over 25 years of
  experience serving the sector, our team is committed to…"* read as a 25-year requirement. It is
  job-DELETING, and larger than T149's measured benefit of 10.
- **Site:** `eligibility/rules.yaml:537` `&company_side_years`, used as `subject_suppressors` at
  `:600`, `:610` and `:620` (searched BEFORE the span).
- **The fix is an AFTER-span suppressor with a NARROWER anchor.** Aliasing `*company_side_years`
  there is **unsafe**: it contains `you\s+will\s+(?:work\s+with|receive|gain|get)`, which after a
  bar suppresses genuine requirements.
- **Known false-positive trap in D-544's sample:** *"Associates Degree … with <2 years pertinent
  experience"* matched the anchor. It must stay a requirement.

### T105 — Bounded heading context for bullet lists (DESIGN DONE; blocks T101 Half A)
- **Design:** `docs/program/DESIGN-T105-heading-context.md`. It uses an out-of-band unit-index →
  heading map, so `split_units`' output stays byte-identical.
- **Why byte-identical matters:** it protects the 1,076 goldens, the anti-reorder lock and
  `abstain_by_adjacent`'s `index + 1` arithmetic.
- **The golden corpus cannot express the shape** (0 of 1,076 rows contain a newline), so a new
  multi-line fixture surface is owed.
- **Once it lands, T101 Half A becomes implementable** (D-544: a JD carries its obligation
  structurally, under a heading).
